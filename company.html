FAQ

Questions brands usually ask.

A few practical points before starting a campaign.

What can brands use Funngro for?

Funngro's current brand page lists promotion, sampling, referrals, influencer marketing, content creation, surveys and insights, app testing, ideation, sales support, loyalty, campus ambassador programs and partnerships.

Who can a brand reach?

Funngro currently describes its audience as young Indians aged 14–25, with campaign matching based on the cohort and work type required.

Do brands have to run only influencer campaigns?

No. The current platform describes several action types beyond influencer activity, including content, sampling, research, product ideation, app testing and promotion.

How do we start?

Use the contact CTA to share your brand, target audience and objective. The Funngro brand page directs companies to email hello@funngro.com to get in touch.
